About Me

Hi, I am Hameer! I am a faculty member in Computer Engineering at Nepal Engineering College, Bhaktapur Nepal.

I earned BE and ME in Computer Engineering from Pokhara University. With interests in teaching, research and the application of computing technologies to real-world problems. My academic and research interests include Data Science, Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Image Processing, Artificial Intelligence and related areas of intelligent computing.

I am particularly interested in developing practical systems that connect academic research with real-world applications while helping students develop strong analytical and programming skills.
